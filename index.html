01 / About

个人简介 Profile

陈巧，湖北荆州人，中山大学管理学院会计学博士。现任长沙理工大学经济与管理学院专任教师、 硕士生导师、财会系副主任，兼任教育部学位论文评审专家、湖南省财务学会会员。

Chen Qiao, a native of Jingzhou, Hubei Province, holds a Ph.D. in Accounting from Sun Yat-sen University. He is currently a Lecturer, Master's Supervisor, and Deputy Head of the Department of Finance and Accounting at the School of Economics and Management, Changsha University of Science and Technology. He also serves as a thesis review expert for the Ministry of Education and a member of the Hunan Provincial Finance Society.

研究领域聚焦资本市场会计与审计、信息披露监管与公司治理、反倾销会计。 相关成果发表于 China Journal of Accounting Research、 Corporate Social Responsibility and Environmental Management、 《审计研究》等国内外 SSCI、CSSCI 期刊。

His research focuses on capital market accounting and auditing, disclosure regulation and corporate governance, and anti-dumping accounting. His work has appeared in leading Chinese and international journals, including China Journal of Accounting Research, Corporate Social Responsibility and Environmental Management, and Auditing Research.

02 / Research

研究方向 Research Areas

从会计信息出发，连接资本市场、监管制度与公司治理。

Starting from accounting information to connect capital markets, regulation, and corporate governance.

01
∿

资本市场会计与审计 Capital Market Accounting and Auditing

研究会计信息在资本市场中的定价、监督与资源配置作用，重点关注行业信息披露、审计质量与审计定价。

Studies the pricing, monitoring, and resource-allocation roles of accounting information, with a focus on industry disclosure, audit quality, and audit pricing.

  • 行业信息披露Industry Information Disclosure
  • 审计质量与审计定价Audit Quality and Audit Pricing
  • 会计信息与资源配置Accounting Information and Resource Allocation
02
⌁

信息披露监管与公司治理 Disclosure Regulation and Corporate Governance

研究强制性信息披露、政府会计监督和公司治理机制，考察制度安排如何影响企业行为与市场效率。

Examines mandatory disclosure, government accounting supervision, and governance mechanisms, and how institutional arrangements affect corporate behavior and market efficiency.

  • 信息披露监管Disclosure Regulation
  • 政府会计监督Government Accounting Supervision
  • 公司治理机制Corporate Governance Mechanisms
03
◇

反倾销会计与企业高质量发展 Anti-dumping Accounting and High-quality Corporate Development

从会计信息证据与融资约束视角，研究企业应对反倾销、提升全要素生产率与出口绩效的机制。

Investigates how accounting evidence and financing constraints shape firms' anti-dumping responses, total factor productivity, and export performance.

  • 反倾销会计Anti-dumping Accounting
  • 融资约束Financing Constraints
  • 全要素生产率Total Factor Productivity
